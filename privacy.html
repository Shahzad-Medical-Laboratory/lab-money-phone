Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Overview

Lab Money Tracker respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy outlines what data we collect, how it is used, and how it is secured.

2. Data We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide the service:

  • Account Data: Your email address and authenticated user identifier.
  • Profile Data: Your business name and preferred display currency.
  • Financial Records: Income entries, expense entries, breakdown items, notes, and transaction dates you input.
  • Technical Metadata: Non-personal device identifiers used solely to prevent edit conflicts.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly for calculating summaries, rendering entries, and managing your money tracking records. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or share your data with advertisers or third parties.

4. Data Isolation & Row Level Security

Every row in the database is automatically tagged with your user ID and guarded by database-level Row Level Security policies. No user can access or view another user's financial records.

5. Deletion & Data Retention

Deleted entries are retained as soft deletes for audit and conflict resolution purposes. When you sign out, all user data is cleared from the device memory and screen so subsequent users cannot view previous records.

6. Security

All communication with Supabase uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Authentication tokens are managed securely in local browser storage, and secret service keys are never stored in client code.

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions regarding your personal information, please contact your laboratory administrator or data privacy coordinator.
